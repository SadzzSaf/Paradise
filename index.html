About Paradise

Built for
Champions.

Paradise Gym was forged from a simple belief — every athlete in the Rio Grande Valley deserves a world-class training environment. We didn't build a wellness center. We built a bodybuilding gym. Heavy iron, elite equipment, and a culture that demands greatness.


Whether you're a first-time lifter or stepping on an NPC stage, this is your home. We are the RGV's most serious training facility — no chrome machines, no excuses, just results. The only thing we require is that you show up hungry.

Common Questions

Got
questions?

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — we offer a free day pass for first-time visitors. Come in, train, and see if Paradise is the right fit. No commitment required. Just contact us to claim yours.

Are there long-term contracts?

No long-term lock-ins here. We offer flexible membership options so you can train without the pressure of a 12-month contract. Ask us about our current available plans.

Is Paradise good for beginners?

Absolutely. We welcome lifters at every level. We have beginners who have never touched a barbell and NPC competitors training side by side. Our coaches can guide you no matter where you start.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Our coaching staff offers personal training, strength coaching, and full transformation programs. Contact us to get matched with the right coach for your goals.

What are the gym hours?

We're open 7 days a week to accommodate all training schedules. Contact us directly for current hours of operation — hours may vary on holidays.

How do I get started?

Simple — fill out the contact form below, call us, or walk in. We'll get you set up with the right membership, a tour of the facility, and your free day pass.

Still have questions?

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you fast. We're here to make joining Paradise as easy as possible.
